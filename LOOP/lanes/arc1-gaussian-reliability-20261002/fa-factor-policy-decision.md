## Mathematical distinction and machine-domain boundary

The unchanged positive transform defines delta>0 and hence psi>c even if the rounded Float64 scalar `c+delta` equals c. Compute derivatives with delta=exp(eta), not the rounded subtraction psi-c. Likewise a finite triangular L_R with positive diagonal defines a positive-definite covariance over real arithmetic even if forming and refactoring its rounded product loses positive definiteness. The retained engineering witnesses and independent higher-precision fixed-coordinate results demonstrate these two mechanisms.

Factor positivity is a domain property, not an accuracy certificate. It does not establish reliable solves, log determinants, analytic gradients or fitted optima. The complete likelihood and final numerical certification below still decide admissibility.

Preserve existing explicit refusals for nonfinite parameters, nonfinite transforms/factors, exponential underflow to zero, overflow, genuinely zero/invalid factor diagonals, invalid design/relationship context, singular fixed-effect information, nonfinite objective/gradient, or failed numerical certification. Do not extend the representable parameter range by secretly evaluating an underflowed/overflowed Float64 transform in BigFloat. Finite mathematical exp(eta) alone does not authorize accepting the old finite-parameter underflow/overflow controls. Freeze the precise numerical-range refusal predicates and test them; a phrase such as 'mathematically PD' cannot waive them. Any required intermediate whose stable computation still underflows/overflows without a declared exact algebraic cancellation remains refused. Exact zeros caused by a genuinely zero relationship eigenvalue are distinct from underflow.

## Consistent likelihood, gradient, reference and selector

The new evaluator must avoid using rounded subtraction or a second Cholesky of materialized L_R*L_R' as the sole domain gate. It must also avoid then recreating the same unstable rounded covariance in its spectral block solve.

One acceptable algebraic route forms each nonnegative-eigenvalue spectral block from covariance factors and obtains a stable triangular factor by orthogonal factorization. For an accepted relationship eigenvalue d>=0, a conceptual block factor is `[sqrt(d)*Lambda, sqrt(d*c)*I, sqrt(d)*Diag(sqrt(delta)), L_R]`; its product is exactly d*G+R in real arithmetic. K0 omits the loading column. This describes the required covariance identity, not permission to change the relationship spectrum, drop small columns or ignore unstable operations. Implementation may use an equivalent stable factor method after independent review. Use triangular solves/log determinants of that representation and retain the full fixed-effect REML correction.

Analytic derivatives continue to differentiate the original coordinates and the same q=-ell/df. Keep both prescribed final central-difference steps, scaled-gradient threshold <=1e-6, FD agreement and independent objective discrepancy <=1e-8 exactly as frozen. Line-search and final-certificate failures remain failures even with positive factors. Preserve every valid intermediate likelihood and all better-unaccepted competitor/nesting guards.

The independent certificate must evaluate the same factor-defined covariance through an independently implemented stable route. Refactoring a rounded matrix that already lost definiteness is not that certificate; reusing the optimized objective without an independent check is not a replacement. A stable factor implementation that still cannot satisfy the existing accuracy thresholds refuses. Do not add unbounded high-precision fallback to every fit merely because independent fixed-coordinate high-precision controls are useful.

K0-derived K1 starts and expanded-reference anchors must carry the same factors/excess rather than round-trip through rounded G/R. The mathematical formulas and frozen starts are unchanged: for a diagonal K0 anchor, the existing g_i-c can be represented by its retained delta_i. Do not add or remove starts because a numerical representation changed. Retain invalid starts without replacement. Expanded reference, both candidate ranks, score reconstruction and same-covariance nesting witnesses must use the declared new representation consistently. Score remains -2*raw_REML_loglik+2p with p=14/18.
