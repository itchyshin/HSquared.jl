## Independent physical certificate

Let e>0 remain fixed, u=a/s0 and v=p/s0, and q=-ell/nu with s0>0 and positive residual degrees of freedom nu. At [a,p,e]=[0,0,e], the residual term makes the covariance positive definite in the valid retained design. Full and same-e centered ell differ by a constant for derivatives.

1. Independently evaluate direct full and same-e centered likelihoods at the returned terminal and exact corner, and verify the existing independent exact-zero intersection has the same coordinates, valid native status and finite scores. All three active centered scores must have spread <=1e-8. Retain full-score spread as a diagnostic. Both winner components and full/centered likelihoods must refer to the exact corner, not the tiny positive terminal. Original pre-continuation scores may be worse; preserve them as history rather than imposing a false equality requirement.
2. At the exact corner evaluate the physical component gradient independently. Require finite s0*dq/da and s0*dq/dp, each strictly >5e-6. A vanishing log-coordinate gradient does not supply this proof. Zero, negative, threshold-equal and nonfinite derivatives refuse the new certificate.
3. Evaluate directions (1,0), (0,1) and (1/2,1/2), each at h=1e-4 and 1e-5, with coordinates s0*h*d and s0*2*h*d and e fixed. Use explicit multiplication in `(-3*q0 + 4*q1 - q2)/(2*h)`. All full and centered evaluations must be valid and finite. On the active centered q scale require both estimates nonnegative, each within 5e-6 of the analytic directional derivative, and their pairwise difference <=5e-6. Preserve full-q estimates separately as diagnostics; never mix these scales or relax a threshold because one looks better. Any certificate probe whose active likelihood exceeds the proposed corner by >1e-8 refuses this candidate's corner certificate.
4. Strictly positive partial derivatives of this smooth objective imply a positive directional derivative along every nonzero feasible first-order direction in the genetic nonnegative quadrant, hence strict local corner behavior. The joint probe is a numerical interaction check. Neither the derivative argument nor a finite probe set proves a global optimum or absence of a better distant point. Keep all existing finite-grid, multi-start and exact-face limits.
