## Frozen model and algorithm

The four-trait genetic DGP has mean vector `[0,1,-1,2]`, `rho∈{0,0.15,0.70}`, column loading vector `L=sqrt(0.5*rho)*[1,1,-1,-1]^T`, uniqueness `psi=0.5*(1-rho)`, and `G=L*L'+Diagonal(psi)` (`diag(G)=0.5`). Residual covariance has diagonal 0.5 and off-diagonal 0.1. N counts observed offspring (80, 160, 320), with N/8 unrelated sires, two unrelated dams per sire, four offspring per dam, unphenotyped parents, and pedigree size 11N/8. Relationship coefficients are 1/0.5/0.25/0 for diagonal/full-sib/half-sib/unrelated. Observations are record-major, `y=vec(Y')`, with `V=B⊗G+I⊗R`, `Xfull=X⊗I4`, and rotation across records `U'⊗I4`.

The optimizer contract uses the exact spectral REML objective and analytic gradients, with BFGS capped at 2,000 iterations per start, optimizer tolerance `1e-8`, and final scaled-gradient infinity norm at most `1e-6`. Selection score is `-2*raw_REML_loglik+2p`, with covariance-parameter counts 14/18 and AIC penalty terms 28/36; ties within `1e-6` favor K0. Expanded ordinary-start reference accepts a best-found shortfall no greater than `1e-4`.

## Profile search contract for implementation

Astra reviewed the following candidate algorithm on 2026-10-02. Its numerical resolution and statistical calibration still require evidence. Method version: `arc1_profile_v1`; confidence level 0.95; likelihood-ratio cutoff `3.841458820694124`. Let `s0` be the ordinary least-squares residual sum of squares divided by the residual degrees of freedom.

For fixed h² or repeatability, profile the total scale analytically and search the remaining allocation on `0:0.02:1`, augmented by `2^-20`, `2^-12`, `2^-8` and their complements. Refine every sampled local maximum with bounded Brent search, allocation tolerance `1e-7`, likelihood tolerance `1e-8`, and at most 2,000 evaluations per bracket. Compare every valid exact endpoint.

For fixed raw variance, search the other two positive components from `s0 * {0.01,0.1,0.5,1,5}²`, including each exact-zero face and their intersection when nonsingular. Each BFGS start allows 2,000 iterations, optimizer tolerance `1e-8`, and final independently checked scaled-gradient infinity norm at most `1e-6`. Positive search bounds are `2^-40*s0` and `1e6*s0`. A competitive bound hit or better unconverged solution makes nuisance optimization unresolved.

Ratio targets start on `0:0.02:1` plus the point estimate, with every gap subdivided to at most 0.005. Refine observed crossings, cutoff-adjacent regions, sampled local maxima, and midpoint-versus-linear likelihood-ratio differences above 0.05 until widths are at most 0.00025. Root tolerance is `1e-6`; allow at most 4,097 distinct target evaluations per target.

Raw-variance targets use zero, the point estimate, and `s0*2^(j/2)` for `j=-40:8`. Subdivide positive gaps to at most one-eighth octave and the first gap from zero to at most `1e-6*s0`. Extend the upper rail by factors of two until four consecutive evaluations are outside the cutoff with decreasing profile likelihood. The hard bound is `1e6*s0`; failure to bracket before the bound gives an explicit refusal. Root tolerance is `1e-6*s0`, with the same 4,097-evaluation cap. This upper-tail screen is finite numerical evidence, not a proof about every point beyond the search domain.

Every likelihood ratio uses one common best-found unrestricted maximum. For this first implementation, a constrained fit exceeding that maximum by more than `1e-7` log-likelihood units refuses the intervals with `point_fit_not_optimal`. It must not silently change the reference maximum for only some targets. Exact ratio-one and zero-residual boundaries are singular for the paired-repeat design and count as analytically excluded boundaries. Zero additive variance, zero permanent-environment variance, h² zero, and repeatability zero use their valid reduced models.

An unresolved target evaluation cannot be interpolated across. Retain observed disconnected components, the searched domain, maximum gaps, refinement history, brackets, tail-screen evidence, and all nuisance statuses. `finite_grid_resolved` describes the declared numerical search only; narrower unsampled components are not ruled out. A formed interval requires resolved, finite, ordered components. This entire algorithm must pass the declared calibration cells before an uncertainty claim is accepted.


## Exact paired-record computation review

Astra independently reviewed this acceleration during S2 implementation after the first full five-target wrapper exceeded its five-minute estimate and was stopped. It changes computation only; the finite-search protocol and acceptance margins remain fixed.

For each of the `m` observed animals, recover its actual two record indices from the incidence matrix. Form orthonormal sum and difference operators `S` and `D`. Take the observed marginal relationship matrix from the inverse of the full precision matrix, retaining unphenotyped ancestors. Never invert only an observed subblock of the precision matrix. If `Aobs = U * Diagonal(d) * U'`, the orthogonal transformation is `T = [U' * S; D]`. Cache `z = T*y` and `H = T*X`, including every fixed-effect column.

The covariance eigenvalues are `v = [2Va*d + 2Vpe + Ve; Ve*ones(m)]`. With `w = 1 ./ v`, `M = H' * Diagonal(w) * H`, `beta = M \ (H' * (w .* z))` and `e = z - H*beta`, raw REML log likelihood is `-0.5 * (sum(log, v) + logdet(M) + sum(w .* e.^2))`. This preserves `reml_omit_2pi`. A cached, parameter-independent OLS shift may remove large fixed means; add its coefficients back to reported estimates. Never compute the quadratic by subtracting two large quadratic forms.

Divide negative likelihood and gradients by `df = 2m-p` only for optimization. Define `r_i = (w_i - w_i^2 * H_i * inv(M) * H_i' - (w_i*e_i)^2)/(2df)`, evaluating leverage through triangular solves. Gradients with respect to `(Va,Vpe,Ve)` are `(sum(2d .* r[1:m]), sum(2r[1:m]), sum(r))`. Log-coordinate gradients multiply by the corresponding positive component. Exact-zero faces omit fixed-zero coordinates. Analytic scale remains the unit-scale residual quadratic divided by `df`; raw likelihood ratios retain the full fixed-effect determinant.

Independent dense-oracle tests must cover likelihood, coefficients, component and log-component gradients, analytic scale, arbitrary record and animal ordering, ancestors, record-specific covariates, large fixed means, zero faces, small positive residual variance and repeated eigenvalues. Source and full interval tests remain unverified until their retained checks pass.

## Invalid optimizer trials

Astra rejected a proposed clamped finite penalty for invalid FA covariance trials because its supplied gradient was inconsistent with its value and its value was not guaranteed to exceed valid objectives. Final fit reevaluation alone cannot repair an inconsistent line search. The implementation must preserve the exact objective on valid proposals, reject invalid trials through domain-aware bracket shrinking, and report a failed start when a valid step cannot be found. No scientific acceptance requirement is weakened by this repair.


### Guarded Hager-Zhang restart contract

The installed LineSearches implementation asserts finite interior secant and bisection evaluations. Its handling of invalid initial or expanded trials does not cover those interior calls. Astra reviewed a repair that preserves exact valid likelihood and gradient values: guard both scalar callbacks, intercept an invalid trial before it reaches the line search, halve the offending step bound, and restart a fresh Hager-Zhang search from the same iterate and direction. Every retried interior evaluation remains guarded. No feasible-interval assumption or artificial penalty is used.

Each line search allows at most 32 domain restarts and 256 callback requests. Record requests, actual objective evaluations, rejected trials and restarts separately. Exhaustion, invalid bounds or an unrepresentable step produce an explicit failed start. Restore objective and gradient caches and scratch coordinates to the original iterate, and raise the line-search failure with step zero. This is necessary because the installed Optim version applies the exception's step even after failure.

Verify each returned step against the true objective and gradient and the same standard or approximate Hager-Zhang Wolfe conditions, with delta 0.1, sigma 0.9 and epsilon 1e-6. Preserve numerical-gradient failure status and distinguish the finite-difference verification tolerance of 5e-6 from the analytic-gradient eligibility threshold of 1e-6. Regression checks must cover invalid interior trials with valid endpoints, invalid values and slopes, exhausted retries, unchanged failed iterates, valid accepted steps and an ordinary-start real fit.

These implementation details still require source tests and inclusion in the canonical execution contract before any scientific study. The previous feasible-ray wrapper failed the tiny fit and is superseded.
