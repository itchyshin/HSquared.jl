## Frozen model and algorithm

The four-trait genetic DGP has mean vector `[0,1,-1,2]`, `rho∈{0,0.15,0.70}`, column loading vector `L=sqrt(0.5*rho)*[1,1,-1,-1]^T`, uniqueness `psi=0.5*(1-rho)`, and `G=L*L'+Diagonal(psi)` (`diag(G)=0.5`). Residual covariance has diagonal 0.5 and off-diagonal 0.1. N counts observed offspring (80, 160, 320), with N/8 unrelated sires, two unrelated dams per sire, four offspring per dam, unphenotyped parents, and pedigree size 11N/8. Relationship coefficients are 1/0.5/0.25/0 for diagonal/full-sib/half-sib/unrelated. Observations are record-major, `y=vec(Y')`, with `V=B⊗G+I⊗R`, `Xfull=X⊗I4`, and rotation across records `U'⊗I4`.

The optimizer contract uses the exact spectral REML objective and analytic gradients, with BFGS capped at 2,000 iterations per start, optimizer tolerance `1e-8`, and final scaled-gradient infinity norm at most `1e-6`. K0 uses its existing start. K1 uses its existing two starts plus 16 deterministic K0-derived starts (eight sign patterns, first sign positive, crossed with `a=0.1,0.7`), `L_i=a*s_i*sqrt(g_i-1e-4)`, `psi_i=g_i-L_i²`, carrying residual covariance forward. Selection score is `-2*raw_REML_loglik+2p`, with covariance penalties 14/18; ties within `1e-6` favor K0. Expanded ordinary-start reference accepts a best-found shortfall no greater than `1e-4`.

The exact-objective core and deterministic checks are in progress under S1; the optimizer dispatch and policy integration remain outstanding. ## Uncertainty and evidence design

Univariate repeatability profiles cover five targets (`Va`, `Vpe`, `Ve`, `h²`, `t`) at triples `(0.3,0.2,0.5)`, `(0.3,0.01,0.69)`, `(0.01,0.3,0.69)` and N=80/160/320. Use exact REML, analytic scale where available, explicit zero reduced models, and LR cutoff `3.841458820694124`; preserve unresolved, singular, unconverged, unbracketed, and disconnected results visibly. Every one of the five targets must meet the formation and all-attempt coverage rules: formation at least 0.99 and a 90% Wilson interval wholly within `[0.92,0.98]`. The repeatability study uses 30 development, 30 pilot, and 400 confirmation attempts per cell. Exact-zero `Va`/`Vpe` checks use 200 N=160 diagnostics each and make no nominal coverage claim.

Selection uses 30 development, 30 pilot, and 400 confirmation attempts per cell across nine cells; expanded reference uses 20 preselected confirmation attempts per cell plus every flagged case. Its K0 split is `{0.05,0.15,0.3,0.5,0.7,0.9}`; K1 has 8 signs × amplitudes `{0.025,0.1,0.25,0.5,0.7,0.9,0.97,0.995}`. Each expanded start uses BFGS 10,000; the top four finite solutions continue through Nelder–Mead 20,000 then BFGS 10,000. Report best found, never global. Pilot numerical screen requires at least 29/30 both candidates available per cell and all expanded-reference checks good; null K1 selection ≤6/30; strong N=320 K1 selection ≥24/30. Failure holds confirmation, without auto-extension. All denominators include failures. Required per-cell Wilson 95% bounds: both-candidates-eligible proportion lower `≥0.90`; null-K1 selection upper `≤0.20`; strong-signal N=320 K1 selection lower `≥0.80`. Weak-signal selection and covariance recovery remain descriptive; no covariance tolerance is introduced.

FA heritability intervals cover four `h²_i=G_ii/(G_ii+R_ii)`. The candidate percentile bootstrap reruns the entire selector for every inner sample. The pilot uses N=160, 30 outer attempts per three signals, B=199; endpoint screening uses 20 designated pilot fits and nested B=499/999; confirmation uses 400 outer attempts per signal and B=499 if qualified. Every required target/cell must have formation at least 0.99 and a 90% Wilson interval for all-attempt coverage wholly within `[0.92,0.98]`; intervals must be finite and ordered. Report formed coverage, all-attempt coverage, refusals, and widths separately.

Development, pilot, and confirmation use namespace `h2-arc1-gaussian-reliability-20261002-v1`; seed is UInt64 from the first 16 hex digits of SHA-256 over UTF-8 canonical pipe-joined `[namespace, programme(fa_rank/fa_interval/repeatability/boundary), phase(development/pilot/confirmation/diagnostic), N, signal-label, outer-index, inner-index, stream(data/bootstrap/reference)]`. Indices are 1-based; inner 0 denotes outer-data generation. Different programmes prevent reuse. S0 freezes the rule; S3 generates and validates exact manifests before any study. Historical seeds may be inspected but never serve as confirmation. A failed pilot holds confirmation; a failed confirmation leaves its gate unmet. Preselect expanded-reference outer indices 1:20/cell; endpoint pilot uses first 20 pooled round-robin signals (null, weak, strong), with nested B=199/499/999 prefixes sharing inner seeds. Pilot/confirmation runs are not evidenced by this planning record.
