## Frozen model and algorithm

The four-trait genetic DGP has mean vector `[0,1,-1,2]`, `rho∈{0,0.15,0.70}`, column loading vector `L=sqrt(0.5*rho)*[1,1,-1,-1]^T`, uniqueness `psi=0.5*(1-rho)`, and `G=L*L'+Diagonal(psi)` (`diag(G)=0.5`). Residual covariance has diagonal 0.5 and off-diagonal 0.1. N counts observed offspring (80, 160, 320), with N/8 unrelated sires, two unrelated dams per sire, four offspring per dam, unphenotyped parents, and pedigree size 11N/8. Relationship coefficients are 1/0.5/0.25/0 for diagonal/full-sib/half-sib/unrelated. Observations are record-major, `y=vec(Y')`, with `V=B⊗G+I⊗R`, `Xfull=X⊗I4`, and rotation across records `U'⊗I4`.

The optimizer contract uses the exact spectral REML objective and analytic gradients, with BFGS capped at 2,000 iterations per start, optimizer tolerance `1e-8`, and final scaled-gradient infinity norm at most `1e-6`. K0 uses its existing start. K1 uses its existing two starts plus 16 deterministic K0-derived starts (eight sign patterns, first sign positive, crossed with `a=0.1,0.7`), `L_i=a*s_i*sqrt(g_i-1e-4)`, `psi_i=g_i-L_i²`, carrying residual covariance forward. Selection score is `-2*raw_REML_loglik+2p`, with covariance-parameter counts 14/18 and AIC penalty terms 28/36; ties within `1e-6` favor K0. Expanded ordinary-start reference accepts a best-found shortfall no greater than `1e-4`.

The exact-objective core and deterministic checks are in progress under S1; the optimizer dispatch and policy integration remain outstanding. ## Exact paired-record computation review

Astra independently reviewed this acceleration during S2 implementation after the first full five-target wrapper exceeded its five-minute estimate and was stopped. It changes computation only; the finite-search protocol and acceptance margins remain fixed.

For each of the `m` observed animals, recover its actual two record indices from the incidence matrix. Form orthonormal sum and difference operators `S` and `D`. Take the observed marginal relationship matrix from the inverse of the full precision matrix, retaining unphenotyped ancestors. Never invert only an observed subblock of the precision matrix. If `Aobs = U * Diagonal(d) * U'`, the orthogonal transformation is `T = [U' * S; D]`. Cache `z = T*y` and `H = T*X`, including every fixed-effect column.

The covariance eigenvalues are `v = [2Va*d + 2Vpe + Ve; Ve*ones(m)]`. With `w = 1 ./ v`, `M = H' * Diagonal(w) * H`, `beta = M \ (H' * (w .* z))` and `e = z - H*beta`, raw REML log likelihood is `-0.5 * (sum(log, v) + logdet(M) + sum(w .* e.^2))`. This preserves `reml_omit_2pi`. A cached, parameter-independent OLS shift may remove large fixed means; add its coefficients back to reported estimates. Never compute the quadratic by subtracting two large quadratic forms.

Divide negative likelihood and gradients by `df = 2m-p` only for optimization. Define `r_i = (w_i - w_i^2 * H_i * inv(M) * H_i' - (w_i*e_i)^2)/(2df)`, evaluating leverage through triangular solves. Gradients with respect to `(Va,Vpe,Ve)` are `(sum(2d .* r[1:m]), sum(2r[1:m]), sum(r))`. Log-coordinate gradients multiply by the corresponding positive component. Exact-zero faces omit fixed-zero coordinates. Analytic scale remains the unit-scale residual quadratic divided by `df`; raw likelihood ratios retain the full fixed-effect determinant.

Independent dense-oracle tests must cover likelihood, coefficients, component and log-component gradients, analytic scale, arbitrary record and animal ordering, ancestors, record-specific covariates, large fixed means, zero faces, small positive residual variance and repeated eigenvalues. Source and full interval tests remain unverified until their retained checks pass.
