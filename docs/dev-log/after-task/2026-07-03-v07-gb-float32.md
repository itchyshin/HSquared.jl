## Evidence (tamia H100, job 360780)

- **Float64 GATE — PASSED:** `precision = Float64` GPU `G` ≡ CPU to **7.3e-15**. The precision kwarg
  did not break the Float64 contract.
- **Float32 accuracy:** absolute `G` error **~1–5e-6**, downstream **GEBV impact ~1e-6** (rel L2
  ~1e-6) — numerically-equivalent for prediction. Large max-rel-error (up to 2.6) = benign
  near-zero-entry artifact. `default` and `pedantic_fp32` math modes IDENTICAL → TF32 not engaged,
  FP32-level accuracy in both (no TF32-vs-FP32 contrast demonstrated).
- **Float32 speedup:** **~1.1–1.4×** (one noisy m=5000 outlier). Modest — SGEMM vs DGEMM without
  tensor cores, diluted by H2D + O(n²) copy-back at n=2000.

## Honesty pins

- Float32 OPT-IN, never the default; Float64 contract unchanged (gate 7e-15). A speed-vs-accuracy
  trade, and the trade is exactly what the run reports — numerically safe but only ~1.2× faster.
- I did NOT demonstrate a TF32 tensor-core Float32 win; the modest speedup is the honest result, and
  the owed-note optimism ("larger speedups") was CORRECTED, not carried forward.
- `V2-GRM-GPU` stays `partial`; NO covered flip; `public_covered_count` UNCHANGED. GPU =
  acceleration, not a new estimand. Machine-specific (H100, n=2000, m≤80k); no competitive/portable
  claim. `Pkg.test()` GREEN (55, stub).
