# Independent method review: automatic Gaussian genetic FA rank design

Date: 2026-10-01. Base: HSquared.jl 28f90e09. Scope: design-only review of the proposed T4, complete-response, pedigree Gaussian REML selector. No fits, tests, simulations, repository edits, capability promotion, or release actions were performed.

## Final verdict: PASS for V0 design closure only

The bounded scientific proposal is viable as an experimental comparison rule. The model, the K=0/K=1 meaning, the nominal parameter counts, the signed score, the matched-floor requirement, and the denominator policy are coherent. I initially held V0 closure for two implementation-critical text gaps: the structural identification check needed the REML contrast space, and a better unconverged FA start needed to trigger the one retry. The owner resolved both in the shared draft during this review. I reinspected the exact changed paragraphs and O8 before issuing this final PASS. This PASS does not cover V1 implementation, numerical performance, selector reliability, R activation or any public claim.

### 1. Structural identification must be checked after removing the fixed-effect space

Contract lines 26 and 98 and validation O8 (line 28) require a design-sensitive kernel-rank check but leave its target ambiguous. The objective at contract lines 36-40 is REML. For an orthonormal contrast matrix Q with Q' X_full = 0, the relevant covariance is Q' V Q. A raw check that Z A Z' differs from I can therefore pass a design that has no G/R separation under the actual likelihood.

Concrete negative witness: one record per animal, four trait intercepts, Z=I, and a valid all-half-sib relationship matrix A=(1-rho)I+rho*11' with rho=1/4. For Q_n'1=0, Q_n' A Q_n=(1-rho)I. With Q=Q_n tensor I_4, the REML contrast covariance is I_(n-1) tensor ((1-rho)G+R). Consequently every admissible change in G can be offset in unstructured R, locally, while leaving the REML likelihood unchanged. Off-diagonal pedigree relatedness exists, but this design remains confounded. This is a stronger witness than A=I and implements the contract's observation that relatedness alone is insufficient.

Resolution verified: revised contract line 26 now requires Q'X_full=0 and the projected Q'V_K Q kernels, states the half-sib counterexample, and requires refusal. Revised validation O8 at line 28 adds the projected half-sib witness and refusal. The numerical singular-value or rank threshold and its scaling remain an implementation acceptance item, frozen before real selection fits. A raw A-vs-I screen would still be insufficient. The existing A=I witness remains.

### 2. Retry the case that the final eligibility policy declares unresolved

Contract line 58 allows one additional FA retry only for failed, unconverged, or materially non-nested initial FA results. Line 62 separately makes the comparison unavailable if a finite unconverged start has loglik more than tau/2 above the chosen converged start, after the one allowed retry. A mixed initial pair can have a converged start, a higher unconverged start, and no nesting failure. It then becomes unavailable without using the retry that was designed to resolve exactly this kind of optimization ambiguity.

Resolution verified: revised contract line 58 adds a better finite unconverged FA attempt exceeding the best eligible converged FA loglik by tau/2 as an explicit trigger for the sole deterministic retry, with the same four-start ceiling. Line 62 retains unavailable selection if the discrepancy survives. Validation O7 already names a manufactured better-unconverged attempt and the one-retry ceiling; its future implementation test should assert that the mixed-start case actually invokes the retry before an unresolved result is returned.

## Scientific checks that pass

- Model and dimension: G_0=diag(psi) with psi_i>c still has positive genetic variance; K counts shared factors, not matrix rank. At T=4, q_G is 4 versus 8, and common unstructured R contributes 10, yielding q_0=14 and q_1=18. The four common intercepts are removed by REML. The score difference D=C_1-C_0=8-2(ell_1-ell_0), so K=1 requires a loglik gain greater than 4 away from ties. This is a nominal generic dimension, not an effective degree of freedom at a singular loading pattern (contract lines 17-22, 32-44; method recommendation lines 7-17).
- Matched floor: the current public diagonal mapping is exp(theta), whereas FA uses c+exp(theta). The selector-only diagonal mapping must share the FA floor for exact covariance nesting; preserving the public diagonal route is the right boundary. At L=0 the models meet, but first derivatives in loading directions vanish, and a conventional four-df chi-square reference is not justified (contract lines 24-28, 64; source src/multivariate.jl:693-723). Drton's primary one-factor singularity analysis is a more directly relevant source for this point than a generic variance-on-boundary analogy: https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0703360 .
- Criterion: restricted likelihoods on the identical response and fixed-effect contrast space can be compared as a predeclared covariance score. Calling this experimental REML AIC, reporting full scores plus D, and excluding predictive-optimality, biological-truth, calibrated-probability and regular-LRT claims is appropriately narrow (contract lines 30-54; validation lines 9-15). The engine includes the REML constant and common log-Cholesky residual coordinates (src/multivariate.jl:660-671, 844-856). The proposal does not claim that ordinary AIC theory validates the singular selector. The mixed-model selection review supports the caution: https://arxiv.org/abs/1306.2427 .
- Numerical tolerances: tau=1e-6 is a predeclared score-unit tie convention, not a calibrated uncertainty interval. At the 1e-8 absolute per-candidate objective-reevaluation gate, the combined discrepancy in D is at most 4e-8 by triangle inequality, leaving a factor-25 gap to tau. This checks objective implementation at returned covariances; it does not bound optimizer suboptimality or all floating-point error, as contract line 46 correctly states. The 1e-8 gate remains conditional on the future moderate-scale pre-run meeting it. The design must stop and revise before a campaign if it does not; no retrospective widening after outcomes.
- Failure and nesting: both candidates must be eligible, a sole surviving candidate cannot win, and a finite nonconverged objective cannot certify an optimum. A floor-matched FA family includes the diagonal covariance at L=0, so an eligible FA loglik below the eligible diagonal by more than tau/2 is an optimization/nesting failure, not evidence against K=1 (contract lines 62-68). The L=0 witness checks objective nesting only; it should not be treated as a successful FA optimization.
- Boundary and units: the fixed absolute c=1e-4 can change the feasible family under rescaling. Reporting per-trait floor distances for both candidates, paired rescaling witnesses, and a boundary-sensitive flag is necessary. A near-floor fit may remain provisionally eligible without implying uniqueness identification (contract lines 15, 68; validation lines 49-53).
- Validation denominators: validation lines 49-63 retain selected K=0, selected K=1, unavailable, interrupted, timeouts and all fit failures over every declared dataset. The primary correct-selection fraction over all declared datasets, plus a separate fraction conditional on selection, prevents favorable attrition. G=0 is labelled fitted-family misspecification; weak-signal cases have no promised winner. The nominal binomial MCSE example, 0.5/sqrt(100)=0.05, is arithmetically correct. This is a descriptive study plan, not a success claim or coverage study.

## Further nonblocking precision

The design should name the criterion-unit versus loglik-unit conversion every time tau/2 is used, to prevent later code from comparing unlike scales. For the local rank-one retry, verify that the nonzero loading perturbation leaves all uniquenesses strictly above c and compare its starting covariance with the matched diagonal target before optimization. The planned independent same-covariance objective oracle validates arithmetic, while a separate independent fit/reference is still needed to judge easy-cell optimization; validation line 61 correctly requests this. The current source can return a finite unconverged FA attempt and reports multiple-start diagnostics (src/multivariate.jl:1097-1165), so the retry-policy gap is operationally relevant.
