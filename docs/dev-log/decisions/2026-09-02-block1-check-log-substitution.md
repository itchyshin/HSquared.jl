## Decision

For Block 1 arcs **B0–B3**, **B5–B6**, and the **Julia pass-3 cluster**, the
`docs/dev-log/check-log.d/` shards dated **2026-09-01** (and the arc-level shards
already cited from those batches) **are** the after-task record.

These arcs ran under overnight / parallel / interrupted-session conditions. The
shards were written at the time and carry the commands and outcomes that mattered.
Writing reflective after-task summaries weeks later would manufacture narrative
content the Definition of Done is designed to prevent.

**Exception already on file:** B4 has a real after-task report
(`hsquared` `docs/dev-log/after-task/2026-09-01-h2-b4-bridge-phase1-after-task.md`).

**A21 after-task record:** the receipt at
`~/local-scratch/h2-a21-fix-receipt.md` (plus the R check-log shard
`docs/dev-log/check-log.d/2026-09-02-h2-a21-rr-grammar-and-accessor-honesty.md`).

**A23 after-task record:** the panel at
`~/local-scratch/h2-a23-d43-completion-panel-2026-09-02.md`.

**From the next arcs onward (A24+, Block 2, and any later Block 1 follow-up
that is not covered above):** standard after-task reports are required again.
This substitution is **narrow and time-bounded** — it does not extend by silence.

---

## Why Option B (not consolidated retroactive reports)

- Check-log shards already exist at arc level for every completed cluster in
  scope (Melissa A22 inventory: 29 campaign shards; 1 formal after-task besides
  A21/A23 receipts).
- Option A (two consolidated per-lane after-task reports) would add polished
  post-hoc narrative and further inflate unpushed ahead counts without improving
  evidence quality.
- Rose G11 closeout may still note the DoD deviation; **this decision document
  is the answer** — the gap is acknowledged, not papered over.

---

## Scope fence (non-negotiable)

- Does **not** waive check-log.d evidence for any arc.
- Does **not** waive after-task reports for future work.
- Does **not** authorise covered flips, G10 signs, registry, version bumps, or push.
- Does **not** change `public_covered_count` or any claim surface.
