## 1. Why fence, not identity (Standard-Tier)

The 2026-07-09 Standard-Tier Covered-Flip Gate
(`hsquared/docs/dev-log/decisions.md`) splits estimands:

- **Component** estimands (`σ²a`, `σ²e`, covariance-matrix entries) are
  gated by an external same-estimand comparator, or by a written
  recovery-substitution when no free same-estimand tool exists.
- **Derived** estimands (`h2_T`, `m2`, `r_am`, `R`, and design-41 §3 #3’s
  `h²` / scale-h²) are **not** comparator-gated. Each needs a
  within-package **identity test** (the derived quantity equals its
  defining function of the covered components) **and** a **locked,
  pinned citation** for that identity. They **may not** be flipped
  `covered` on component comparator or component recovery evidence
  alone.

design-41 §3 #3 restates the same rule for this lane.

The n=240 single-step gate **predeclared** the split

> **h²** = σ²a / (σ²a+σ²e) is **reported, not gated.** … do not flip h²
