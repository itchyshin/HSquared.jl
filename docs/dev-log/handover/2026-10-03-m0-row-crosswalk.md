The current R control surface also has several documentation and error-contract mismatches to carry into its Arc 1 leaf. `R/fa-rank-selection.R:11–15,50–55,82` accepts optimizer policies `arc1_v1` and `arc1_v2`, while some validation messages mention only `arc1_v1`. `ROADMAP.md:181–183` and `docs/design/18-structured-covariance-r-control.md:242–245` still describe automatic rank as future work. `man/hs_control.Rd:301–322` does not document the complete current v2 bootstrap and v3/v4 profile controls. These are source-backed stale surfaces, not evidence that the experimental route has calibration or broader grammar support.

The two Julia matrix-free methods are distinct acceptance routes even though the design ledgers group them under one capability family: `fit_matrix_free_reml` is the single-effect MC-EM REML method (`V1-MATFREE-REML`), while `fit_multi_effect_mc_reml` is the arbitrary-N multi-effect Monte Carlo REML method (`V3-NEFFECT-MATFREE-FIT`). Their fixtures, approximation targets, same-estimand comparators, scale cells and failure denominators must be reported separately. R's direct-target refusal is boundary evidence only. Both R `target = "multi_effect", scale_method = "auto"` and `target = "repeatability", scale_method = "auto"` can reach the arbitrary-N method above the dense-infeasible threshold. Existing live R fits exercise the exact sparse branch; mock metadata checks do not establish actual Monte Carlo dispatch. M0-19b therefore needs a required-live above-threshold fixture for each R entry route asserting stochastic-likelihood metadata, finite MCSE and provenance, result/failure parity, reproducibility or controlled Monte Carlo error, and accuracy against the declared comparator.
