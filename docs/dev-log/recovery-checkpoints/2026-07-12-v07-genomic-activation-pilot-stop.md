A 432-seed diagnostic pilot over the preregistered cells, generated by the pre-repair Julia-only

## Audit boundary

Fisher/Noether and Grace found that the generation harness at `1f91763d` did not yet qualify as an
authoritative cross-twin activation harness: its initial Julia summary used raw pilot SD rather than
the already-frozen upper bound, campaign manifests were overwriteable, resume validation was too
weak, and the simulation called Julia construction plus supplied-Q fitting directly rather than the
public R formula route. The independent R recomputation caught the SD mismatch before any
confirmation launch. Therefore these 432 rows are banked as a **Julia construction + supplied-Q
diagnostic pilot**, not as end-to-end public-route recovery evidence.

The diagnostic is still decision-relevant in the conservative direction: the same covered Julia
estimator behind the candidate route failed the pilot convergence/precision gates. Harness repairs
are for the next discriminating experiment; they do not convert this stopped pilot into activation
evidence and do not justify rerunning confirmation.

## Claim boundary and next experiment

Nothing is promoted. `V2-GREML` remains covered only for its existing supplied-precision estimator;
the R marker activation remains partial/held; `public_covered_count` remains 5.
